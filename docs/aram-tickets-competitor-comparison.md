# ARAM Tickets, Cvent and Zoho Backstage

**Internal comparison · 14 October 2026**
Competitor statements below are based on the linked official public product pages. Availability and plan limits may vary by region and package. ARAM Tickets entries reflect features found in the current application; “not verified” is not a roadmap commitment.

| Area | ARAM Tickets — current product | Cvent | Zoho Backstage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Event websites | Event website editor and public event pages are implemented. | Branded, responsive registration sites with drag-and-drop design [C1]. | Drag-and-drop builder; event details, sessions, speaker bios and sponsors can appear on the site [Z1]. |
| Registration and ticketing | Registration and ticket configuration/records are implemented. Online payment processing is not configured; paid checkout is blocked. | Registration tools and Cvent Payments [C1]. | Ticket classes and customizable registration forms [Z2]. |
| QR check-in and access | QR scanning and event access-control tools are implemented. | OnArrival supports QR, name or barcode check-in, badges and session attendance/access controls [C2]. | Check-in and badging are listed; QR scanning and session-level access controls were not verified [Z3]. |
| Speakers and people | Speaker, attendee and people-management areas are implemented. | Speaker-management workflows were not verified in the reviewed sources. | Speaker counts are plan-specific [Z3]. |
| Analytics and reporting | Event analytics are implemented; breadth varies by available event data. | Dashboards, UTM traffic monitoring, feedback, registration and portfolio reporting [C1, C3]. | Basic and advanced event analytics are listed by plan [Z3]. |
| Team permissions | Workspace membership is used; a user-facing, fine-grained role/permission manager was not verified. | Specific team roles or permissions were not verified in the reviewed sources. | Custom team roles are listed for Ultimate [Z3]. |
| Payment support | No payment provider is connected; paid ticket checkout is unavailable. | Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, onsite readers and multiple currencies are described [C1]. | Payment gateways and offline payments are described; pricing lists 0% ticketing commission [Z2, Z3]. |
| Pricing model | Free/Professional/Enterprise plan definitions exist in the application; current public prices and purchase terms require confirmation. Paid ticket checkout is unavailable. | Public pricing details were not verified; Cvent directs prospects to discuss rates [C4]. | Free, subscription and pay-per-event options; exact prices vary and were not recorded here [Z3]. |
| Audience and differentiators | Event organizers and teams; current scope centers on event websites, registration/tickets, people, QR check-in and analytics. No scale or customer-count claims are made. | OnArrival describes self-service for smaller/recurring events and full-service support for larger or more complex events [C2]. | Plans are positioned for individuals and small teams through event professionals, organizations and agencies [Z3]. |

**Roadmap boundary:** No unsupported ARAM Tickets feature is represented as planned. Payment processing and finer-grained team permissions are not verified as implemented or committed roadmap items; confirm product plans before external sales use.
